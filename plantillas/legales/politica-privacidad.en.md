## 2. Data we process, purposes and legal bases

We only process personal data where there is a valid legal basis for doing so.
For each processing activity we state what data is processed, for what purpose,
on what legal basis, who receives it and how long it is kept.

<!-- {{PROCESSING_ACTIVITIES_TABLE}} · CAPA 3 · DEDUCIDO · ver revisión en ES -->

### Enquiries sent through the contact form

- **Data:** name, email address, telephone number (optional) and the content of
  your message.
- **Purpose:** managing and responding to the enquiries you send us through the
  contact form.
- **Legal basis:** your consent, given by ticking the box on the contact form,
  and steps taken at your request prior to entering into a contract where your
  enquiry has that aim.
- **Recipients:** Vercel, which hosts the Website and runs the form submission,
  and Brevo, which delivers the message to us by email. Both act as data
  processors.
- **Retention:** messages are not stored on the Website: they are delivered to
  our inbox and kept there for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and,
  thereafter, for the periods required by law in order to address any
  liabilities that may arise.

Please send only the data needed to deal with your enquiry. Do not include
special categories of data (health, beliefs, etc.) or confidential information
that is not essential for us to answer you.

### Security of the contact form and the Website

<!-- CAPA 3 · DEDUCIDO · REVISAR (ver ES) -->

- **Data:** IP address and technical signals from your browser, such as browser
  type, screen resolution and interaction timing.
- **Purpose:** checking that form submissions come from a person and not from an
  automated bot, limiting abusive submissions and keeping the Website secure and
  working.
- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in keeping the Website secure and
  protecting it against abuse.
- **Recipients:** Cloudflare, which provides the security check (Cloudflare
  Turnstile), and Vercel, as hosting provider. Both act as data processors.
- **Retention:** this data is not stored on the Website; the providers process
  it for as long as needed to provide the security service.

The Cloudflare Turnstile check does not use cookies and is not used to track
you.

### Measuring how the Website is used (only if you accept analytics cookies)

- **Data:** pages visited, time spent on the Website, approximate location
  (country or city), device and browser type, and analytics cookie identifiers.
  It is used in aggregate and does not identify you directly.
- **Purpose:** producing aggregate statistics on visits in order to improve the
  Website.
- **Legal basis:** your consent, given through the cookie panel, which you can
  withdraw at any time from "Privacy preferences" at the foot of every page.
  Withdrawing it does not affect the lawfulness of prior processing.
- **Recipients:** Google Ireland Limited, which provides the Google Analytics
  service as a data processor.
- **Retention:** analytics data is kept for no longer than
  {{ANALYTICS_RETENTION}}.
