# Cookies policy

{{COMPANY_LEGAL_NAME}} uses cookies and similar technologies on {{WEBSITE_URL}}.
This policy explains what they are, which ones are used and how you can manage
them. No cookie that requires consent is installed until you accept it.

## 1. What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies make it possible to store information on the
user's device or to access information already stored there. A cookie is a
small file downloaded to your device when you visit certain web pages;
alongside cookies, equivalent technologies such as local storage may be used
where relevant.

## 2. Types of cookie by purpose

<!-- CAPA 3 · DEDUCIDO: la frase final dice si la web la usa (ver ES). -->

- **Strictly necessary.** Essential for the site to work. They do not require
  consent. Used on this website.
- **Preferences.** Remember certain choices made by the user. Not used on this
  website.
- **Analytics.** Used to measure how the site is used in order to improve it.
  Used on this website only with your consent.
- **Advertising.** Used to measure or personalise advertising. Not used on this
  website.
- **Third-party.** Used by external services embedded in the site, such as
  videos or maps. Not used on this website.
